# Using Orbit

Everything in Orbit is done with the Fire TV remote. This guide assumes Orbit is installed ([SETUP.md](SETUP.md)) and has been opened once.

## The remote

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Arrows | Move the gold focus between tabs, rows and cards |
| Select (centre) | Open what is focused: play a trailer, open a film in its streaming app, start radio, open a journey |
| Back | Go one step back: stop a trailer, close a menu, leave Home Mode or a journey; in Cinema it returns to For You |
| Menu (three lines) | Switch between Cinema and the live channel wall |
| Home | Amazon's home. Fire OS does not let any app replace it; Orbit comes back the next time the TV wakes |

## The first time

A new install opens in setup. It takes about three minutes:

1. **Your name**, typed on the on-screen keyboard. Hold Select on a letter, or use the "à é" key, for accents.
2. **Where is home?** Move through the countries (type to jump) and pick up to three home places.
3. **Your languages, and where you live now.** The first language you pick leads films, greetings, radio and news. Confirm the city Orbit guessed, or choose "Somewhere else".
4. **What you love.** Football, cooking, jazz, space, anything. Move Right on a love to say more (a team, a cuisine). "Search anything" takes any word. You can skip this; Orbit learns as you watch.
5. **Summary**, then **Start**.

Give it a few minutes after Start: films, live sport and news for your choices arrive in the background.

## Your home (Cinema)

The top bar has your tabs:

- **For You**: the hero picks one film or show for you and says why; rows below mix what is on tonight, live sport you follow, home news, and your loves.
- **Your films tab** (for example Nollywood or Sandalwood): films and series in your main language, by genre.
- **Your loves**: up to three tabs for the loves you use most, each with shows, news, videos and live events about it.
- **Journeys** and **Apps**.

Rest on a title for a few seconds to see its trailer. Select opens the streaming app that carries it in your country and tells you the title to search for there. If none of your apps has it, Select plays the trailer, or Orbit says it is not streaming on your apps.

Orbit learns from what you open and how long you watch. When it notices you watching something you have not added as a love, it suggests it once ("Orbit noticed").

## Who's watching

Move Up to the top bar, then Right past the last tab to the circle with your initial, and press Select. The menu lists:

- **Who's watching**: everyone on this TV. Select a person to switch to them. Each person has their own home, picks and history.
- **Add a person**: run setup for someone new (up to six people on one TV).
- **Change my setup**: change your home, languages or loves.

## Journeys

Choose **Journeys** in the top bar to travel anywhere:

- The globe turns as you move along the shelves: live cams, places waking up at sunrise, places like ones you loved, and **Your homes**.
- **Search** finds any city or town.
- Select a place to go there: walks, drives, food, festivals, nature, stays and live cams, with the place's own time and weather, and the sound of the footage. Up and Down change how you travel; Left and Right pick what plays next. Back returns to the globe.

## Home Mode

On For You, move down to the **Home** row at the bottom and select **Feels like <your home country>**. Home Mode shows your home's time, a greeting, the weather and a photograph of your home town, and festival days. Its rows below hold live channels from home, home radio, home news, and More (switch to another person's home, or go back to Orbit). Home Mode is silent until you choose a radio station or a channel. Back returns to Cinema.

## Live channels and radio

- **Menu** opens the channel wall: live channels from your home country, the scores you follow along the top, and the apps on this TV. Select plays a channel or opens an app; Menu returns to Cinema.
- Radio cards (in Home Mode and on music loves) play when selected; select again to stop.

## The screensaver

If you set it up ([SETUP.md, step 6](SETUP.md#6-permissions-and-the-screensaver)), the TV's screensaver becomes the Earth seen from orbit, with the real sun and city lights and the score of a match you follow.

## Privacy, in one line

Your people, picks and history stay on the TV; Orbit has no account and no server. Details: [README, Privacy](../README.md#privacy).

## Removing Orbit

On the TV: Settings > Applications > Manage Installed Applications > Orbit > Uninstall. From your computer: `adb uninstall app.orbittv`. Either way this deletes everything Orbit stored. To restore the Fire TV screensaver, see [SETUP.md, step 6](SETUP.md#6-permissions-and-the-screensaver).
